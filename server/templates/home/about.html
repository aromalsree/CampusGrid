OUR PURPOSE

Championing the Circular Campus Economy

Every semester, thousands of dollars worth of functional graphing calculators, textbooks, lab coats, and monitors end up gathering dust in dorm closets or thrown away during graduation move-out. Meanwhile, incoming students face escalating book bills and expensive tech requirements.

CampusGrid bridges this divide. By making local student reuse effortless, transparent, and direct, we empower universities to foster circular sustainability, reduce e-waste, and significantly lighten the financial burden of higher education.

HELP & CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading, renting, and verifying your identity on CampusGrid.

Ask the CampusGrid Team
Who can use CampusGrid?

CampusGrid is engineered primarily for verified students, researchers, and campus faculty. Registering with an institutional email (.edu, .ac.in, .edu.in) awards you a Verified Student badge and full privileges to list, rent, and coordinate peer handovers.

Does CampusGrid charge any platform fees?

No. CampusGrid never charges transaction fees or takes a commission cut from student exchanges. 100% of the proceeds go directly from peer to peer during physical handover.

How do I stay safe during campus meetups?

Always arrange handovers in busy, well-lit university locations such as the central library foyer, student center, campus cafeteria, or academic department lounges. Inspect all items thoroughly before completing the transaction, and use our pre-filled WhatsApp handover notes for a documented interaction trail.

Can I rent items instead of buying them outright?

Yes. Sellers can configure flexible rental pricing across daily, weekly, or semester-long periods. This makes short-term access to lab equipment, reference textbooks, or specialized project components accessible without paying full purchase prices.

What items are permitted on the marketplace?

Approved items include textbooks, study notes, graphing calculators, lab coats, laptops, monitors, dorm furniture, campus bicycles, and academic electronics. Dangerous goods, non-academic contraband, and prohibited substances are strictly disallowed.

How do I report an issue or fraudulent listing?

You can visit our Contact page or flag the listing directly. Our administration team continuously monitors reported items and takes immediate action against fraudulent or non-compliant accounts.
