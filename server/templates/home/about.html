About CampusGrid

Empowering student communities with a trusted, campus-first marketplace for buying, selling, renting, and sharing academic resources.

Campus-First Design

Built specifically for university ecosystems. Every feature considers campus logistics — handover spots, semester timelines, and student budgets.

Verified Student Community

Academic email verification (.edu, .ac.in, .edu.in) creates a trusted network. Verified badges on profiles and listings reduce scams and build confidence.

Multi-Modal Listings

Sell, rent, offer services, or share digital assets — all in one platform. Flexible pricing, rental periods, and condition ratings for every item type.

Smart Comparison Engine

Side-by-side hardware specs comparison for laptops, tablets, and electronics. Automatic winner highlighting for processor, RAM, storage, battery health, and price.

Need Board (Reverse Marketplace)

Students post what they need with budgets. Automated matching notifies sellers when their listings match open requests — demand drives supply.

WhatsApp Handover Integration

One-click WhatsApp deep links pre-filled with item details, price, and campus meetup notes. Secure peer-to-peer coordination without exposing contact info.

Why CampusGrid?

Student marketplaces on WhatsApp, Discord, and Facebook Groups are chaotic, unsafe, and lack accountability. CampusGrid brings structure, trust, and campus-specific features to peer-to-peer exchange.

Zero platform fees for students Academic email verification Semester-aware rental cycles Protected digital downloads

Our Mission

CampusGrid was built to solve a simple problem: students need a better way to exchange resources within their campus community. Whether it's a graduating senior passing down textbooks, a CS student selling their laptop, or a freshman needing a calculator for exams — the current solutions are fragmented, unsafe, and inefficient.

We believe that by combining academic verification, campus-aware logistics, and purpose-built tools (like hardware comparison and need-based matching), we can create a marketplace that truly serves students — not advertisers, not resellers, but the campus community itself.

CampusGrid is open-source, student-focused, and built with modern web technologies. Join us in building the future of campus commerce.

Ask the CampusGrid team
Who can use CampusGrid?

CampusGrid is designed for verified students and staff. Use your academic email when creating an account so your campus identity can be recognized.

Does CampusGrid charge platform fees?

No. CampusGrid does not charge students platform fees for buying, selling, renting, or sharing resources with peers.

How do I stay safe when meeting another student?

Review profiles and listing details before agreeing to a handover, use busy campus locations, and never share passwords or payment details unnecessarily.

Can I rent items instead of buying them?

Yes. Listings can include rental periods such as a week, month, or semester, making short-term access to equipment more practical.

What can I list on the marketplace?

You can list electronics, textbooks, academic notes, lab gear, room essentials, bicycles, and other useful campus resources.

How can I report a problem or ask for help?

Visit our Contact page and send the team a message with the relevant listing or account details. We will follow up as soon as possible.
